# Between the Rain and Four O’clock

Original instrumental score for scene 6, the first café meeting. 169.412 seconds (2:49), 68 BPM, 48 bars in 4/4, looping. Written melody and repeating eight-bar harmonic phrases move between A minor and C major, with major sevenths, ninths and suspended dominant colours. Six eight-bar sections vary the melody’s register and density rather than introducing unrelated notes. Sampled grand piano (installed TimGM6mb General MIDI bank), soft arpeggiated answers, warm low-pass/saturation and a barely audible kick pulse. No vocals or borrowed song melody.

`scripts/compose-cafe.py` is the reproducible score and rendering recipe. Ogg Vorbis stores the exact sample duration without MP3 encoder padding. Two passes through the sampled instrument settle reverb before the exported cycle. An 8ms boundary blend suppresses a waveform discontinuity. The scene defaults to a quiet 36% mix, multiplied by the player's master volume; adjust to taste in Scenes advanced.

The soundtrack attaches to the whole café scene, including its background sub-scenes. It continues through dialogue, background changes, volume changes and pause/resume, and stops on leaving the scene. Existing local projects receive the new track once, retaining other tracks and artwork. After installation, deleting the track from a scene is respected.
