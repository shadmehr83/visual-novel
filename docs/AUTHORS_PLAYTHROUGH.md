# Author’s playthrough

Click **Author’s playthrough** beside **Play story** to play with the regular editor controls unlocked. You can also turn it on during a normal playthrough without restarting. The highlighted author button switches the tools off again while leaving the story at the same point.

The dialogue editor follows the actual scene and dialogue reached through your choices. **Edit current dialogue** opens the dialogue panel and focuses the text being spoken. Edit the speaker, dialogue, reply text, route labels or author note; the running scene updates immediately. The current authored phone message updates too. Previously sent custom replies and earlier messages remain playthrough history.

Lights, rain, camera, sound, background and other regular scene controls remain available. They target the currently running scene, including after a route crosses into another scene. To move a local light, use **Lighting → Move light with mouse**; a placement click does not also advance the story. Advanced graph work, node deletion, choice deletion and route/state wiring remain in the stopped editor.

Edits change your project and use the existing local autosave, Undo, Redo and project export system. They are not temporary playback overrides. Use **Back to editor** to continue editing the same dialogue. As before, export or publish your project to transfer manual edits to another device; local autosave alone does not publish to GitHub.

Automatic dialogue advancement and timed actions wait while a text or numeric field is focused. Move focus out of the field to resume; automatic dialogue gets its full delay after editing. Effects and sound can continue while you adjust them. The ordinary pause menu remains available for a complete pause. **Restart story** restarts the route without discarding your authored changes.

Normal **Play story** and exported player files retain the standard player experience. Author’s playthrough is a studio debugging mode.
