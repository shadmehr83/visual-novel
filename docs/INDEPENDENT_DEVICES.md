# Stillframe 2.5: independent devices

Advanced devices → Dialogue & replies → **Dialogue placement** chooses automatic chat speakers, story dialogue outside the device, or explicit texting. **Enable texting only in a dialogue range** adds start/end selectors. Per-dialogue placement overrides the range. Home/feed navigation never advances a hidden conversation.

Opening a device no longer turns story choices into outgoing messages. New messages require an active chat and texting eligibility; old exploration choices are hidden from chat history. In the opening bedroom, narration stays outside the device. Actual Maria conversations in subsequent scenes retain their authored texting behavior.

Look at the monitor → **Interact with the computer** opens an independent desktop view. Step back returns to the monitor; Back to the room returns to exploration. Look at the window opens the new rainy city view and returns through the same dialogue graph. No additional main scenes are created.

Both close-ups have their own glass masks rather than borrowing bedroom masks: monitor windows above/beside the screen, and two glass panes in the window close-up. Animated rain and glass streaks are clipped to those panes. Rain tapping on glass is included in Sound and assigned to the window dialogue.

Compatible saved First Match projects receive this focused update with a recovery copy. Main backgrounds, custom text and café effects are preserved. Existing explicit placement choices remain intact. New view effects and audio travel with project exports.
